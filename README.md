A 1024×1024 infographic summing up Thursday 19 November 2026 in six
stories, drawn by `generate_image.py` (Python standard library only)
and copied to `/output/entry.png` by `main.ts` (Node 22, standard
library only).

Six stories: the first-ever Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral summit in
Shenzhen; the ICJ's genocide ruling against Myanmar; the third Kerch
Bridge strike in four days; Anthropic's record $81bn IPO; Cyclone
Akvan's evacuation of 300,000 people in Odisha; and Trump holding off
on new Iran strikes until 2027. Also today: the US chip rule, Israel
ending its Gaza raid, and COP31 in its final stage. Every fact was
checked against live reporting from 19 November 2026 (AP, Reuters,
CNN, NPR, Le Monde, The Hindu, Axios, and others).

Entry #3 (final) for the news-image-20261119 bounty: the refined
edition's uniform light grid and biggest type, with the lead story's
body carrying the summit's ~90-minute length.
